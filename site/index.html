Henry und Hugo

Die Reise einer Freundschaft

Written by Christin Ritschel · Illustrated by Paiwan Bootvicha

  • Picture book
  • Ages 4-9
  • 29 pages
  • German
  • Paperback · 2023

Henry, a curious caterpillar, and Hugo, an adventurous snail, spend their days exploring a dreamy meadow in Sonnental, until Henry vanishes without a trace. A gentle story about change, loss and a friendship that grows even deeper. Dreamy pictures and large letters make it ideal for reading aloud and for first readers.
